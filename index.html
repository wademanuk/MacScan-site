MacScan

Scan cards and magazines on your Mac, with originals stored in your Google Drive.

MacScan is a personal macOS scanning app. It captures card fronts and magazine sheets, supports page review, cropping and rotation, and creates image exports and searchable magazine PDFs.

Your scans, in your account

Connecting Google Drive lets MacScan upload originals, finished images, PDFs and batch metadata directly from your Mac to your own account. It checks uploads before removing temporary originals. Small review previews and batch metadata remain on the Mac; pending uploads remain locally until delivery is verified.

MacScan requests Google's limited drive.file permission. It uses files it creates or that you explicitly make available to it. It does not request access to every file in your Drive. The app does not automatically share uploaded files with other people.

Personal app

This is a locally installed personal build. There is no public download or hosted scanning service on this website. Your scanner driver and Google account are configured separately.

For support, use the maintainer's support email shown on the Google consent screen.
