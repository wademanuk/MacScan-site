Terms of use

Effective 3 October 2026. MacScan is a personal desktop scanning tool. This website describes the app; it does not offer a public download or a hosted scanning service.

Using the app

Use MacScan only for material you are authorised to scan, store and export. You retain your rights to your own content. Scanning does not grant rights to reproduce, distribute or publish third-party material.

You choose whether to connect your Google account and whether a batch uses local or cloud storage. You are responsible for your Google account, available Drive space, file sharing settings and scanner setup. Google Drive and scanner software are separate services with their own terms.

Review and storage

Review image quality, page order, crops and recognised text before relying on an export. Optical character recognition can make mistakes. Cloud originals are removed from the app's temporary local storage only after the app verifies delivery and the batch manifest. Interrupted or failed delivery can leave pending files locally. Maintain any separate backup you require.

This is a personal build with limitations documented in the app's setup and validation guides. It does not promise uninterrupted operation, perfect OCR or recovery of files that have been deleted from all available locations.

Privacy and support

The privacy policy explains the app's processing and Google Drive access. For support, contact the maintainer using the support email shown on the Google consent screen.
