Privacy policy

Effective 3 October 2026. This policy describes the personal MacScan desktop app and its optional Google Drive connection.

Data accessed and purpose

MacScan processes the images you scan or import, page order and edits, recognised text, batch titles and export settings. Processing and optical character recognition run on your Mac. When you connect Drive, MacScan reads the connected account's email address and permission identifier to display the account and keep batches associated with it.

MacScan requests only https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. It creates batch folders, uploads originals and exports, reads file metadata and checksums to verify delivery, updates batch manifests, and downloads accessible originals for full-quality exports or restoration. It does not request unrestricted access to your Drive.

Storage and sharing

Images, PDFs, recognised text in exports, batch metadata and upload receipts are stored in your Google Drive when you choose cloud storage. Transfers go directly between your Mac and Google's services over HTTPS. There is no MacScan application server receiving copies, and the app does not send scan content to the maintainer, advertisers or analytics services. The app does not automatically grant other people access to your uploads. Your own Drive sharing choices and Google's policies still apply.

Google OAuth client configuration and sign-in tokens are stored in macOS Keychain on your Mac. Small previews, batch metadata and receipts remain locally. Pending originals and unfinished exports are retained until successful delivery is verified. Exporting may temporarily download originals and create output files; these working files are removed after successful cloud export. Locally requested exports, imported source files, operating-system backups and snapshots may retain additional copies.

Retention and control

Cloud scans remain in your Drive until you remove them. Disconnecting in MacScan removes its saved sign-in tokens on that Mac; it does not delete cloud scans or the imported client configuration. You can revoke MacScan access in your Google account's third-party connections settings. You can delete app-created files through Drive and remove local batch files from the MacScan application-support folder after closing the app. Drive trash, backups and Google's retention rules may affect when copies disappear.

Google API data use

MacScan's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the requested scanning, storage, verification, export and restoration features. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train general-purpose AI models.

Website and contact

This information website contains no sign-in form, analytics script or scan upload feature. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes under its privacy policy. For questions or help removing local data, contact the maintainer using the support email shown on the Google consent screen.
